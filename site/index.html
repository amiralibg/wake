The trail

Tabs hid where you've been. The trail shows it.

  1. One page fills the windowStart anywhere. A single column takes the whole window.
  2. Links open to the rightClick a link and the page lands beside the one you came from.
  3. Two share, more scrollTwo columns split the window. Open a third and the trail slides along.
  4. Keep goingEvery hop adds a column. Nothing closes behind you.
  5. Resize one columnDrag its edge. Its neighbours keep their size.
  6. Scroll backYour path is still there, in the order you took it.

  1. Download the disk image

    Open Wake.dmg and drag Wake into Applications.

  2. Clear the quarantine flag, once

    Wake isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID yet, so macOS blocks a downloaded copy with "can't be opened" or "is damaged". This command removes the flag macOS puts on downloaded files.

    xattr -cr /Applications/Wake.app

    Rather not use Terminal? Try to open Wake, then choose Open Anyway in System Settings › Privacy & Security.

  3. Import from your old browser

    The welcome tour sets your look and search engine. Then File › Import from Another Browser brings over history, cookies and site storage.

  4. Updates arrive on their own

    Wake checks once a day and installs a new version when you say so. You can also check from Wake › Check for Updates.
